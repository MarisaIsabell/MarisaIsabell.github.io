I'm Marisa Isabell Keller,
a computational biologist fascinated by microbes. Our evolutionary oldest friends, invisible partners, and important fellows.

I am a Marie Skłodowska Curie postdoctoral researcher in the group of Torben Hansen at the Novo Nordisk Foundation Center for Basic Metabolic Research (CBMR), Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences, University of Copenhagen.

My research focuses on human health, leveraging computational biology, metagenomics, and multi-omics approaches to understand the role of human and microbiome genetics in health and disease.

Research

Human-microbiome symbiosis represents one of the most fundamental relationships in biology. Through computational, molecular, and multi-omics biology, I investigate how our human and microbial genes influence human health and disease. I carry out this research in close collaboration with clinical experts, always aiming to improve disease management and provide foundations for translational advances.
